Common questions

Before you call

What does a buyer's agent do in Melbourne?

A buyer's agent works only for the buyer. Across Melbourne that means searching the north, south east, east and west, including off-market and pre-market properties, attending inspections on your behalf, running due diligence on inspections, overlays, amenities, school zones and council planning, valuing the property independently, and negotiating or bidding for you.

Is a buyer's agent worth it for a first home buyer?

Yes — and arguably it matters most the first time. This is the largest purchase you have made, you are doing it for the first time, and the person on the other side of the table negotiates every week. With an experienced professional beside you, you buy at the right price and on the right terms, with every check done before you commit, and you spend your weekends looking at homes worth seeing instead of losing auctions. That is the difference between a purchase you are still happy with in five years and one you are not.

Which suburbs do you cover?

All of Melbourne. We have specialists across each corner of the city — Tanuj in the south east, Arshad in the west, Nirvan in the north and Abhimaan in the east — and between them the team has purchased over 1,000 properties. Wherever you are looking, get in touch for a confidential conversation and we will tell you how we can help.

Can you find homes that aren't listed online?

Yes, and it is one of the main reasons buyers come to us. We source over 200 off-market and pre-market properties through our agent network across Melbourne, so you are seeing homes that never reach a portal. Deals we have shortlisted for our clients sit behind that again — those we walk you through in person.

Do you work with property investors?

Yes. As well as buying homes for people to live in, the wider Pathway Property team buys investment property for clients around the country. Tell us what you are trying to achieve and we will put you with the right person on the team.

How long does it take to buy a home with a buyer's agent?

It depends on your brief and how tight the market is when you start. We'll give you a realistic range on the first call based on what you're after and what you can spend, rather than a number that just sounds good on a website.

What if I've already found a property I like?

Bring it to us. We can give you an independent read on what it is worth, review the contract and the section 32, flag anything that deserves a closer look, and negotiate or bid on your behalf. Even if you are close to the finish line, a second set of eyes before you sign is worth the conversation.
