Why a separate office

Buying a home is not just an investment.

The money matters. You still want to buy well, and you still want to be able to sell it one day without wincing. But an investor never has to care whether the back yard gets afternoon sun, how bad the school run is at 8:15, or whether the estate three streets over is finished or still a construction site.

Those things don't show up in a yield calculation. They're most of what makes a house worth living in — and they're the reason we run this office separately, with an agent who buys in this corridor every week rather than one who covers the whole country.

Common questions

Before you call

You're new in the south east. Why should I use you?

The office is new. The people aren't, and neither is the process. Tanuj grew up in the corridor and has been involved in over $100 million of property transactions; the Pathway Property team behind him has been buying nationally for years. What we can't offer yet is a stack of local reviews, which is why the first call is free and why you're under no obligation after it.

What's the difference between you and a real estate agent?

The selling agent at that open home is paid by the vendor and is legally acting for them. Their job is to get the highest price. We're engaged by you, paid by you, and never take a fee from a seller — so our job is the opposite one.

Do you work with investors?

Not through this office. If you're buying an investment property, the national Pathway Property team handles that and does it well — we'll introduce you.

How long does it usually take?

It depends on your brief and how tight the market is when you start. We'll give you a realistic range on the first call based on what you're after and what you can spend, rather than a number that sounds good on a website.

What if I've already found a property I like?

Tell us on the call. Depending on where it's up to we may be able to value it, review the contract and negotiate on it — or tell you honestly that you're far enough along to finish it yourself.

Do you cover suburbs outside the corridor?

The south east is where this office buys. If you're looking further afield, say so early and we'll point you to whoever is the better fit, inside Pathway Property or outside it.
