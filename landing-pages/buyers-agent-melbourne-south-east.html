Common questions

Before you call

What does a buyer's agent do in Melbourne's south east?

A buyer's agent works only for the buyer. Across Melbourne's south east and east that means searching from Brighton and Glen Waverley through to Pakenham, including off-market and pre-market homes, attending inspections on your behalf, running due diligence on building, pest, flood, zoning and planning, valuing the property independently, and negotiating or bidding for you. Everyone else at that open home is paid by the seller.

How much does a buyer's agent cost in Melbourne?

Fees vary by agency and by how much of the process you want handled. Some charge a flat fee, others a percentage of the purchase price. We'll walk you through exactly what ours looks like on the first call, before you commit to anything — it's a 15-minute conversation and it's free.

Is a buyer's agent worth it for a first home buyer?

Often, yes, because first home buyers are the most outgunned people in the room. You're doing this once; the selling agent does it weekly. The value shows up in three places: not overpaying, not buying something with a problem you didn't spot, and not spending six months of weekends losing auctions. If your situation doesn't warrant it, we'll tell you that on the call.

Which suburbs do you cover?

Glen Waverley, Oakleigh, Brighton and Ringwood through to Dandenong, Berwick, Narre Warren, Cranbourne and Pakenham, plus the suburbs between them including Officer, Clyde, Hallam, Hampton Park, Beaconsfield and Lynbrook. Looking elsewhere in Melbourne? Tell us where you're looking and we'll let you know how we can help.

What's the difference between a buyer's agent and a real estate agent?

The selling agent is engaged and paid by the vendor and is legally acting for them. Their job is to get the highest price. A buyer's agent is engaged and paid by you and never takes a fee from a seller, so the job is the opposite one.

Can you find homes that aren't listed online?

Yes. We work off-market and pre-market listings through the agent network across Melbourne, and if the right home still isn't available we'll approach owners directly on your behalf. It doesn't work every time. When it does, you're the only buyer in the conversation.

Do you work with property investors?

Not through this office. If you're buying an investment property, the national Pathway Property team handles that — we'll introduce you.

How long does it take to buy a home with a buyer's agent?

It depends on your brief and how tight the market is when you start. We'll give you a realistic range on the first call based on what you're after and what you can spend, rather than a number that just sounds good on a website.

What if I've already found a property I like?

Tell us on the call. Depending on where things are up to we may be able to value it, review the contract and negotiate on it — or tell you honestly that you're far enough along to finish it yourself.
