How is Islamic finance different from a normal loan?

No interest is charged on money lent. The funder buys or co-owns the asset with you and earns rent or a profit margin instead, through structures like Ijarah, Diminishing Musharakah and Murabaha.

Can I switch my existing loan?

In most cases, yes. We compare your options, show you the real numbers and manage the switch for you.

Will it cost me more than a bank loan?

It depends on you and the funder. The gap is often smaller than people expect, and comparing is how you find out.
