FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Sira a bank?

No. We're an Islamic finance broker. We compare Shariah-compliant products from multiple funders and help you choose.

How does Islamic finance work?

Funders charge rent or profit instead of interest, using structures like Ijarah, Diminishing Musharakah and Murabaha.

Is it more expensive than conventional finance?

It depends on you and the funder. The gap is often smaller than people expect - comparing is how you find out.

Can I switch from conventional finance?

Yes. We compare your options, show you the real numbers and manage the switch.
