    Is Islamic finance really different from a normal loan?

    Yes. Instead of charging interest on money lent, the funder buys or co-owns the asset with you and charges rent or a profit margin, using structures like Ijarah, Diminishing Musharakah and Murabaha.

    Can I switch my current loan to Islamic finance?

    Usually, yes. We compare your options, show you the real numbers and manage the switch from start to finish.

    Is it more expensive than conventional finance?

    It depends on you and the funder. The gap is often smaller than people expect, and comparing is how you find out.

    Is Sira a bank?

    What does it cost to use Sira?
