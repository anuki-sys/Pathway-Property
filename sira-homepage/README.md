# Sira Finance homepage options

Two standalone, responsive redesigns of the Sira homepage (Home V2), revised against the
10 Nov 2026 website review with Muneer and Nirvan. Open either file in a browser.

- `option-a.html` - Option A: close to Home V2. Four service tiles in the hero, text-only
  "What brings you here?", dark mission band, tabbed "What we finance" with switching photo.
- `option-b.html` - Option B: mission-led. Dark hero with an "I want to..." sentence builder,
  four service blocks breaking out of it, dictionary-style Sira definition, four plain finance
  columns (no toggles), moving review wall, sticky Book a call / WhatsApp bar on phones.

Placeholders: anything in [square brackets] still needs real content (stats from Muneer, bios,
podcast name, video links, verbatim Google reviews). Teal/sand boxes labelled "Photo:" mark where
a photo goes and describe the shot. Team photos and logos are taken from the Home V2 design.
The lead form is a stand-in for the CRM web form embed.
