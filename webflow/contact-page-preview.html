Preview - Contact page with the GoHighLevel form

Everything outside the form (the cream card, its 50px padding and 30px corners, the heading, the intro line, the off-white outer container) is rendered here with the exact values pulled from the live Webflow site, so this is what the page will look like.

  • The form itself loads live from GoHighLevel. Until the custom CSS is pasted into the GoHighLevel form builder, it will still show its own default styling - white fill, boxed inputs, default button.
  • Once that CSS is in, the fields become underline-only on transparent, the type becomes Plus Jakarta Sans in Midnight #090D2B, and the button becomes the midnight pill.
  • Nothing has been published. The Webflow site is unchanged until you say go.
