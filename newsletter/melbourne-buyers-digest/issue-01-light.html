Melbourne bottomed near 54% in early July and has climbed every month since. If you have been reading national coverage about the worst auction results in years, that is not your market. What has changed is who you are bidding against: investors have largely left established Melbourne, listings are running above average, and owner-occupiers have not rushed in to take their place.
 
One thing worth doing
The cash rate is 4.35% and the Reserve Bank decides again on 29 September, with markets pricing about an 81% chance of a rise. NAB expects it on the 29th; ANZ, CBA and Westpac expect November. Ask your broker what your approval is worth at 4.6%.
