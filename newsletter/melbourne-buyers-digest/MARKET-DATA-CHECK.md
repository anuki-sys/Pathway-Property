# Data check, 16 September 2026

The Issue 01 market figures came from the original brief, which recorded
`data_verified_on: 2026-09-10`. They did not survive checking.

## The brief was wrong, and wrong in direction

| Claim in the brief | What the sources say |
|---|---|
| "Melbourne cleared **55%** over the week to 5 September" | Melbourne cleared **64.2%** in the week to 12 September. Houses 63.4%, units 62.9%. Melbourne has been tracking consistently above 60% |
| "The same week last year it was **71%**" | The 71.1% figure is the **national** average for the same week last year, not Melbourne's |
| Direction: falling | Direction: **rising**. Melbourne bottomed near 54% in early July and has climbed since |
| National **46.1%**, down from 47.3% | 46.1% was the week to 5 September. The week to 12 September was **51.9%**, i.e. up |

The brief's 55% looks like early July's Melbourne number, roughly ten weeks
stale by the send date.

**This matters beyond the numbers.** The whole editorial angle was built on the
premise that Melbourne is softening, and that is not what is happening. The
headline, the subject lines and the "you have more time than you think" framing
were all resting on a false reading.

## What is actually true, and is a better story

- **Melbourne is outperforming the country by about 12 points.** 64.2% against
  51.9% nationally. A Melbourne buyer reading national "worst results in years"
  coverage is reading the wrong market.
- **Investors have largely left established Melbourne.** Attributed to the
  May 2026 Federal Budget grandfathering negative gearing and cutting the
  capital gains tax discount from 50% to 25% on new acquisitions.
- **Advertised listings are above average**, so buyers have more choice than
  they have had in years, and owner-occupiers have not rushed in to fill the
  gap investors left.
- **The rate decision is the live risk.** Cash rate 4.35%, held in August, next
  decision 29 September. Markets price roughly an 81% chance of a rise. NAB
  expects September; ANZ, CBA and Westpac expect November.
- Inflation: CPI 3.5% for the year to July, down from 3.8% in June. Trimmed
  mean 3.6%, unchanged. Both above the 2 to 3% target.

## Also corrected

  pieces, the largest presentation the house has staged in Australia. NGV
  Friday Nights run every Friday to 2 October.
