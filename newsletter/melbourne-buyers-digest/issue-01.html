What this means for you.
About half the homes that went to auction in Melbourne last weekend didn’t sell on the day. A year ago, closer to three in four did.

If open homes have felt like you are always the one being hurried, that is what has changed. A home that passes in is not a failure. It is a conversation.
One thing worth doing this week
The Reserve Bank meets on 29 September and rates could move. Ask your broker what your budget becomes if they do. Knowing the number takes the fear out of the room.
