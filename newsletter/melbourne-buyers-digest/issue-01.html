Good to know
Cooling off does not exist at auction.
3 days
Private sale
None
At auction
0.2%
Or $100 to walk
On a private sale you get 3 clear business days from the day you sign to walk away in writing, and it costs you 0.2% of the price or $100, whichever is greater. On an $800,000 home, $1,600.
Buy at auction, on auction day, or within three days either side of one, and there is no cooling off at all. The bid is final.
So every check you were going to do afterwards has to happen before you raise your hand. Contract reviewed, finance confirmed, building and pest read.
