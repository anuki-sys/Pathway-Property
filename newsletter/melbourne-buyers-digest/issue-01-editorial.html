The Melbourne Buyer’s Digest
 
The market
Week to 12 Sep
Melbourne is clearing 64%. The country is on 52%. That gap is the whole story.
64.2%
of Melbourne auctions sold under the hammer last weekend. Houses 63.4%, units 62.9%.
Melbourne bottomed near 54% in early July and has climbed every month since. If you have been reading national coverage about the worst auction results in years, that is not your market.
What has actually changed is who you are bidding against. Investors have largely left established Melbourne, advertised listings are running above average, and owner-occupiers have not rushed in to take their place. More to choose from, fewer hands in the air.

National average, same week PropertyUpdate. Up from 46.1% the week before 51.9%

Next rate decision Markets price about an 81% chance of a rise 29 Sep
One job this week. NAB expects a rise on the 29th; ANZ, CBA and Westpac expect November. Ask your broker what your approval is worth at 4.6% before you commit to a price.
What’s on
This week in Melbourne
School holidays start on Saturday, so this weekend is already a quiet one.
Term 3 ends Friday 18 September and the break runs to Sunday 4 October. Thinner inspection numbers for a fortnight, and fewer auctions to compete at. Saturday shouldn’t only be open homes anyway.
