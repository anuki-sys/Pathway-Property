Strategy of the month
Why property investing, and why now
Property is one of the few assets most people can buy with the bank's help. Used well, that leverage lets a deposit grow into a meaningful position over time, while tenants help cover the holding costs.
What's driving it
■ Long-term growth: well-located property has historically grown in value over the long run, and that growth applies to the full value of the property, not just the deposit.
■ Rental demand: rental vacancy sits at [#]% nationally, so quality rentals lease quickly and rent helps cover the holding costs.
■ Equity to build on: as a property grows in value, the equity can fund the next purchase. That's how most of our clients build a portfolio.
■ Tax structure: interest, depreciation and holding costs can be deductible, so it's worth setting up the ownership structure with an accountant before buying.
Suits clients who
Have stable income and $[#]k+ in deposit or equity to work with
Can hold for the long term (7-10+ years) and ride out short-term cycles
Less suited to clients who
Need the money back within a few years
Don't have a buffer for vacancies, rate rises or repairs
