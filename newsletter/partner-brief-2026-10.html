Strategy of the month
Why rooming houses, and why now
A rooming house is one property let room by room to individual tenants (in VIC, 4+ unrelated residents). That means several rents from a single title instead of one.
What's driving it
■ Cashflow: typically neutral to positive from day one - rare in today's rate environment
■ Rental demand: single renters, students and key workers are the most squeezed segment of the rental market
■ Diversification: balances a growth-heavy portfolio that's eating into serviceability
Suits clients who
Need income now, or have borrowing capacity capped by negatively geared properties
Have $[#]k+ in deposit or equity and are comfortable with a more hands-on asset
Less suited to clients who
Want maximum capital growth or a "set and forget" asset
Rely on negative gearing for tax benefits
