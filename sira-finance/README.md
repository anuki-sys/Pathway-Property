# Sira Finance - website revamp

A buildable design for the full rebuild of sirafinance.com.au, following
`00-BUILD-INSTRUCTIONS.md` and the per-page content drafts.

Nothing here touches the live Webflow site. The Sira Webflow site
(`698a6fa14505a9ea9ac2198e`) has not been modified and nothing has been published.

## Running it

```
python3 build.py               # src/ -> dist/
python3 tools/check_copy.py    # brand and compliance copy rules
node tools/screenshot.js dist/index.html /tmp/home
```

`build.py` inlines the CSS and the fonts into every page, so any file in `dist/`
can be opened, emailed or dropped on a phone on its own with nothing to load.

## Layout

```
assets/             brand assets, generated by tools/extract_brand_assets.py
brand/source/       the supplied logo artwork, the origin of everything in assets/
css/sira.css        design system. The only place a colour is defined
css/fonts.css       generated by tools/fetch_fonts.py. Review build only
src/layout.html     page shell
src/partials/       header, footer, draft banner
src/pages/          one file per page, body only, with a meta block on top
schema/             JSON-LD per page, ready to paste into the Webflow page head
dist/               generated. Do not edit
```

## How this ports into Webflow

The CSS is written **desktop-first using Webflow's three breakpoints**
(991, 767, 479) and nothing else. That is not a stylistic choice: Webflow's
WHTML builder only accepts those exact media queries, so each `<section>` here
can be handed straight to it with its matching CSS and lands as real Webflow
elements rather than an embed.

Before porting:

1. Create the colours as Webflow variables. They are the `:root` block at the
   top of `css/sira.css`.
2. Plus Jakarta Sans is already enabled on the Sira Webflow site, so
   `css/fonts.css` is not needed there.
3. Upload `assets/sira-logo-lockup-*.svg` and `assets/sira-wavy-texture.webp`
   as Webflow assets and point the header, footer and hero at them.
4. Strip the review-mode helpers: `.draft-bar`, `.needs-input`, and every
   `data-needs-input` attribute. Those exist for this review only.
5. Paste `schema/<page>.json` into the page's head. Webflow does not output
   JSON-LD natively.

Page folders for the nested URLs (`/islamic-finance/...`) need the Webflow
Designer app open. The headless API can create pages and elements but not
folders.

## Typography and colour

Both were taken from the brand rather than from `00-BUILD-INSTRUCTIONS.md`,
at the client's direction. Worth recording, because it is a deliberate
departure from that document.

**Typeface: Plus Jakarta Sans**, not Fraunces and Inter. It is the typeface in
the logo artwork (ExtraLight and SemiBold) and the only family on the current
site, where h1 is 700 at -2.5px tracking and body is 16px at 1.7. The site
follows that: tight negative tracking on display sizes, 17px body at 1.65.

The lockup pairs SemiBold "sira" with ExtraLight "finance". That pairing is
reused as a display device, so a headline reads as one semibold clause and one
extralight clause. It is the `.light` span inside an h1 or h2.

**Colour: sampled from the logo artwork.** Its ground runs from `#0C3330`
through a dominant `#154D47` to `#276860`, over a cream of `#F5F1EB`, and it
carries no warm accent at all.

| Brief | Logo-derived | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Elm `#206E65` | `--pine #2A6F66` | demoted to hover and accents |
| Cyprus `#0F3E3A` | `--deep #0C3330` | footer and deep surfaces |
| (none) | `--forest #154D47` | the dominant green, now the primary surface |
| Alabaster `#F5F2EB` | `--cream #F5F1EB` | the artwork's own cream |
| Black green `#010A09` | `--ink #0A1C1A` | warmer, sits better on cream |
| Blanched almond, Ecru | dropped | the logo has no warm accent |

Blanched almond carried secondary text on dark and Ecru carried accent rules.
Those roles are now cream at reduced opacity and plain hairlines, which is what
keeps the palette as tight as the logo's.

There are no drop shadows anywhere. Surfaces separate by a one-step tone change
and a hairline.

## Brand assets

`tools/extract_brand_assets.py` derives everything in `assets/` from
`brand/source/sira-logo-wavy.pdf`. Re-run it if the artwork is ever reissued.

The lockup is the real vector art lifted out of that file, not a redraw. The
wordmark glyphs and the mark carry different fills in the source, so each can
be recoloured independently without touching the geometry. That gives the two
variants: cream on green, and green mark with ink wordmark on cream.

The mark reads as a cream square with a sail curved out of it. The sail is
negative space, so on a cream page the whole path is filled green instead and
the page shows through as the sail.

The wavy silk behind the logo is the same file's embedded raster. It sits over
the green at low opacity with a screen blend, which is how it works in the
original: the highlights lift the green rather than darkening it. It is used on
the hero and the closing band only.

## Components built so far

Scenario tile, trust bar, service card, step block, comparison table, cost
table, FAQ accordion, testimonial card, guide card, inline CTA band, funder
panel strip, lite YouTube embed. Every one is a class in `css/sira.css` and
gets reused, not rebuilt, on the remaining pages.

Two of them are built on a shared `.matrix`: a grid with a 1px gap over a
line-coloured ground, so every cell shares one true hairline rather than each
drawing its own border.

The comparison table, cost table and guide card are not used on the home page.
They are built now because the guides and the broker comparison page need them.

## Copy rules are enforced, not just documented

`tools/check_copy.py` fails the build on: em and en dashes, "loan", "lending",
"lender", "borrower", "mortgage" outside "mortgage manager", "halal loan",
superlatives, US spellings, ALL CAPS outside a short acronym list, meta titles
over 60 characters, meta descriptions over 155, and images without alt text.

One deliberate exception is whitelisted: the phrase "home loan" inside the FAQ
question *"How is Islamic home finance different from a conventional home
loan?"*. That is exception (a) in the language rule, quoting a searcher's
question verbatim before reframing it. It needs sign-off.
